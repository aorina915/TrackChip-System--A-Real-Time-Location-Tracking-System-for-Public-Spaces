## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: How long should the entire video be?
**A:** Exactly 10 minutes (1 min intro + 1 objective + 1 features + 1 architecture + 5 demo + 1 conclusion). Use the timing in DEMO_RECORDING_GUIDE.md Part 6.

### Q: Can I pause between sections?
**A:** Yes! It's actually easier to record each section separately in Google Vids, then piece them together. Mistakes in one section won't ruin the whole video.

### Q: What if I make a mistake during recording?
**A:** Stop, take a breath, and start that section again. Google Vids allows editing, and you can always re-record problem sections.

### Q: Do I need to memorize the scripts?
**A:** No! Read the scripts naturally but you don't need to memorize them word-for-word. Make them sound conversational in your own voice.

### Q: What if the system is slow during demo?
**A:** Close other applications, reduce map zoom, or prepare a backup scenario. See troubleshooting in DEMO_RECORDING_GUIDE.md Part 8.

### Q: Can I record on my phone?
**A:** You can, but recording from a desktop/laptop through Google Vids gives better quality and easier screen sharing.

### Q: Do I need a high-end microphone?
**A:** No, but record in a quiet room. Most laptops have adequate built-in mics. Test audio quality beforehand.

### Q: How do I submit the video?
**A:** After recording:
1. Click Share in Google Vids
2. Copy the link
3. Set permission to "Commenter"
4. Submit through the Google Form provided in DEMO_RECORDING_GUIDE.md Part 8

## 📞 Support & Troubleshooting

### If the system won't start:
See **DEMO_PRE_RECORDING_CHECKLIST.md** - Troubleshooting section

### If a feature won't work:
1. Restart the specific service (backend or frontend)
2. Check the browser console for error messages
3. Verify credentials: `admin` / `trackchip123`
4. Clear browser cache and try again

### If recording quality is poor:
- Check lighting (especially for on-camera sections)
- Test audio levels beforehand
- Use quiet environment
- Close other applications
- Record in incognito mode to reduce browser overhead

### If you run out of time during demo:
Focus on features in this priority order:
1. Login & Dashboard (essential)
2. Tracking Map (essential)
3. Geofence Management (important)
4. Alerts (important)
5. Activity Logs (nice to have)
